# Rewoven Account Deletion

This page explains how to request deletion of your Rewoven account and associated data.

## How to Request Deletion
Email us at: studentfirebase256@gmail.com

Include the following in your email:
- Subject: Rewoven Account Deletion Request
- Your account email address
- Your full name (if used in the app)

We will confirm your request and delete your account data within 7 business days.

## Data That Will Be Deleted
- Account profile (name and email)
- Authentication credentials
- Any data stored in your account

## Data Retention
- We do not keep your data after deletion.
