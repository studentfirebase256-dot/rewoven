About Rewoven

Rewoven is an innovative educational platform that makes learning engaging, competitive, and rewarding. Designed for students who want to master knowledge through interactive quizzes, instant feedback, and structured progression.

Key Features

  • Interactive Quiz System — 4-option multiple-choice questions with instant feedback and detailed explanations
  • Sequential Learning Path — Unlock categories as you progress, mastering one topic before advancing
  • Progress Tracking — Detailed analytics, accuracy rates, streaks, and performance insights
  • Competitive Leaderboard — Compete with students worldwide with real-time rankings
  • Rich Learning Content — Comprehensive articles with reading progress tracking
  • Secure & Synced — Your progress syncs across devices with privacy-focused design

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I create an account?

Download Rewoven from the App Store, open the app, and tap "Sign Up" to create your account with email or continue with Apple/Google.

Q: How do I unlock new categories?

Complete quizzes in your current unlocked categories. Once you achieve the required score, the next category automatically unlocks.

Q: Is my progress saved?

Yes! Your progress is automatically saved and synced to your account. You can continue learning on any device.

Q: How does the leaderboard work?

Your ranking is based on total points earned from quizzes. Answer correctly and maintain streaks to climb higher!

Q: Can I reset my progress?

Contact our support team if you need to reset your progress. We'll help you start fresh.

Q: Is Rewoven free to use?

Rewoven offers free access to core features. Some premium content may require a subscription.
